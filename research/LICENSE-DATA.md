# Data licence and attribution

This directory produces derived statistics from the **JRA-3Q** reanalysis
(Japanese Reanalysis for Three Quarters of a Century), produced by the Japan
Meteorological Agency (JMA) and distributed by NSF NCAR GDEX as dataset
`d640000`.

## Licence

JRA-3Q is distributed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)** licence.

Three consequences that apply to everything produced under `climatology/`:

1. **Non-commercial only.** These products may not be used for commercial
   purposes.
2. **ShareAlike.** The derived climatology is a derivative work. If it is
   redistributed it must carry CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — *not* the licence of the rest
   of this repository.
3. **Attribution required.** The citation below must accompany any
   redistribution or publication.

Because of (2), the outputs of this pipeline are deliberately kept out of the
web application's build. See `climatology/README.md`.
